How it works

From the first lead to the review.

  1. 01

    Avyro takes the lead

    You add the lead and set a follow-up date. Avyro emails them that day.

  2. 02

    Velto, Rovyn, and Orvyn carry the job

    Velto books and reminds. Rovyn follows the quote until there is a reply. Orvyn reminds on the invoice and confirms when it is paid.

  3. 03

    Nexro and Ravelo bring them back

    Nexro contacts inactive customers and asks for referrals. Ravelo requests the review and keeps unhappy feedback private first.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is in Starter, Growth, and the full stack?

Starter is one module of your choice. Growth is any three modules. The full stack includes all six: Avyro, Velto, Rovyn, Orvyn, Nexro, and Ravelo.

Can I buy one module?

Yes. Starter is one module of your choice: Avyro, Velto, Rovyn, Orvyn, Nexro, or Ravelo.

Is the full stack checkout live?

Yes. One module is €39 per month. Growth is €79 per month for any three modules. The full stack is €149 per month for all six. Tax is not included.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes — you can add modules or move up a plan anytime, no lock-in contract.

What happens if I go over my contact limit?

New records stop for the rest of the month. Upgrade to Growth or the full stack to raise the limit. There is no extra charge.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Each plan starts with 7 days free. A card is required, and billing starts when the trial ends.
